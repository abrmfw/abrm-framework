The Governance Gap

Traditional security frameworks were designed for users, applications, and infrastructure. Autonomous AI systems introduce a fundamentally different operational entity: software capable of reasoning, acting, transacting, and delegating tasks without direct human execution.

Core Control Domains

01

Agentic Blast Radius

A quantitative measure defining the maximum operational, informational, financial, infrastructural and other impact an autonomous agent may exert.

02

Micro-Attestation

Continuous verification of intent, authorization, context, and execution prior to high-impact autonomous actions.

03

Atomic Containment

Runtime isolation boundaries designed to prevent cascading failures and uncontrolled propagation of autonomous actions.

Governance Principles

No autonomous system should operate without a measurable, certified and secure blast radius.
Trust must be continuously validated at runtime.
No high-impact action should execute without attestation.
Accountability cannot be delegated to software.
